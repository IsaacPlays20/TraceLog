About TraceLog

TraceLog is a website for logging, sharing, and discovering your interesting natural places and activities. Log pins, hikes, and other activities. Discover, review, and create a collection of visited hidden gems. We belive all this should be free unlike other mapping sites and we are also fully open source. Get outside and explore!

Powerful Features

Everything you need to log, share, and discover the outdoors in a single application. No payment or subscription required!

🌍

Discover

Find on our map or search and filter to find where your next adventure could take you.

📍

Drop Pins

Mark locations with specific categories like Nature, History, or Landmarks. Add photos and notes to remember the details.

🏃

Track Activities

Trace and plan your walks, hikes, runs, and cycle routes. View elevation profiles and distance statistics in real-time.

🔴

Record live Activities

Record your acitivity live as you move and get realtime stats and information.

🗺️

Plot your Activities

Plot your own routes using the activity trace tool to plan out your next adventure.

🗒️

My Log & Bucket List

Save all your visited locations in your own logbook to remember and bucket places to save for the future.

⭐

Rate and Review

Comment and rate pins and activities to share your experience and find other highly rated locations and activities.

🏕️

Campsite Booking & Pricing

Register campsites with starting price rates and direct links to Pitchup or alternative booking portals.

🅿️

Car Parking

Find where you can leave your car for the day and get info on the car parks facilities.

Easily import routes from GPX files to load existing paths, or export your saved activities to share with other devices or software.

Flyover

Watch your activity from overhead as you follow where your next adventure will take you.

🗺️

Auto snapping

When planning an activity, auto snap your line to roads and footpaths.

Project Timeline

Currently the covered area focuses on the Peak District National Park. See our progress history and what's planned next on our roadmap.

V1.0 - Release

This version introduced the ability to drop interactive pins, write notes, and start building a personal logbook of visited locations.

V2.0 - Personalization

Introduction of activity tracing for walking, hiking, running, and cycling. Account and comments launched as well as My Log to track your own journeys.

V3.0 - General Improvements & Launch Website

Added rich location data, detailed facility tags, an improved mobile experience, and robust cloud syncing tailored for local adventurers.

General Improvements & Growing Userbase

Aim to grow our users to create more content to discover and add more features to help you discover new locations easier.

Future - Expanding to the World

Taking TraceLog global. Our next major leap involves expanding our curated discoveries, introducing advanced routing features, integrating more social/community review aspects, and bringing the full TraceLog experience to explorers worldwide.
